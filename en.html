Yermis Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · Updated October 18, 2026 · App version: 1.0.0 (includes online play, beta)

In short: your progress is stored only on your phone. For online play (beta) we use Unity services that process an anonymous identifier, your IP address to connect phones, and the name you choose to play with. We do not ask for your real name, email or an account. We do not sell data or use it for advertising, and the game has no ads and no purchases. Playing against the computer works offline.

Who we are

Yermis is an Android game made by an independent developer from Colombia. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

What the game stores

So you can pick up where you left off, the game keeps a file only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. That file contains:

  • Your progress: matches won in the Golden Cap Cup (Copa Tapa de Oro), stars, collected caps and unlocked characters.
  • Your settings: volume, vibration, graphics quality, difficulty and whether you prefer to play by day or by night.
  • The character you chose and whether you have already seen the intro and the practice round.
  • The name you chose for online play, your player code (the anonymous Unity identifier, shown in Settings) and the last Remote Config answer (whether online play is on).

That file stays on your phone: we do not send it to any server and we cannot see it (only the name you chose leaves it, when you play online). Uninstalling the game or clearing its data from Android settings deletes it.

Online play (beta) and Unity services

To play online with friends, and to know whether online play is available, Yermis uses three Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies), acting as our service provider and processing data on our behalf:

  • Unity Authentication (anonymous account): creates a random player identifier, with no name, email or password. Other services use it to know the request comes from the game. It is kept until deleted. (Unity documentation)
  • Unity Relay: connects the phones in a room. To do so it processes each phone's IP address and the player identifier; Unity keeps them for 30 days. (Unity documentation)
  • Unity Remote Config: lets us turn online play on or off without releasing a new version. The game checks it when it opens and when you enter ONLINE; each check sends the IP address, an installation identifier, the player identifier and basic phone details (model, Android version, language, memory, screen and app version). Remote Config uses them on the spot to answer and does not store them. (Unity documentation)

In addition, the name you choose for online play (up to 12 letters) is shown to the other players in your room. It travels between phones through Relay and is not stored on any server. You do not have to use your real name; if the name contains profanity, the game replaces it with a random one (for example, "Jugador 4821").

Online play has no text or voice chat.

Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS and DTLS). This data is only used to make online play work: it is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else. More details in Unity's privacy policy for game players.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number or password.
  • We do not use your precise location (GPS), contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • We do not use the advertising ID or track what you do in other apps.
  • There are no analytics of our own, no ads and no purchases.

Internet and permissions

Against the computer, the game is fully playable offline. It uses the internet for online play and to check whether online play is available (Remote Config). The game also uses the vibration permission for vibration effects, which you can turn off in the settings.

Google Play may collect technical device information on its own (for example, crash reports) under its own privacy policy. That is handled by Google, not by the game.

How to ask us to delete your Yermis data

Steps:

  1. Open Yermis and go to Ajustes (Settings).
  2. Under "Tu código de jugador" (your player code), tap COPIAR (copy).
  3. Write to santiagobernalb@hotmail.com with the subject "Borrar mis datos de Yermis" and paste the code in the message.

What is deleted: your anonymous Unity identifier (the player code) and everything linked to it in the Unity services the game uses (Authentication, Relay and Remote Config).

What is kept: nothing on servers. Your progress (cup, caps, characters and settings) is only on your phone: it is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data from Android settings.

Time frame: we delete it within 30 days at most of receiving your email, and we reply when it is done.

If Settings shows "Sin código todavía" (no code yet), your phone never connected to Unity services and there is nothing to delete outside it. If your version of the game does not show the code, update it from Google Play.

Children

Yermis is not directed at children under 13. We do not ask anyone for personal data; online play only uses the anonymous identifier, the IP address to connect and the name each player chooses.

Future changes

If a future version adds anything else that uses data (for example analytics to improve the game, or ads), we will update this page before releasing that version, explain which data is used, why, who it is shared with and how to ask for its deletion, and change the date above.
